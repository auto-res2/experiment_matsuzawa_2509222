# Experimental Design

## Experiment Strategy
Experiment 1 – Target-stream curvature drift benchmark
Objective: Show that SA-KFAC + rank-grow is essential when the dominant curvature directions themselves shift.
Protocol
1. Data stream   a) ImageNet-C (severity 1→5 noise only; little change of principal directions)   b) ImageNet-3DCC (continuous random yaw & pitch; large change of cross-channel correlations) – 50 000 frames, chronological order.
2. Models   ResNet-50-BN.
3. Variants   • APTA full   • APTA-no-grow (rank fixed =4)   • PROTA (fixed =4, no Oja)   • Tent-5 (BN only)   • Source frozen.
4. Measurements (per 1000 frames)   a) Top-1 error   b) Average effective rank rₑ (Σ λ>1 % total energy)   c) Additional memory (MB)   d) Running cosine between source and refined eigenvectors.
Expected outcome
• On ImageNet-C all variants similar (≤0.3 pp); on 3DCC APTA full retains ≤+5 pp over source after 30 k frames whereas PROTA degrades by ≥9 pp and Tent by ≥12 pp. • rₑ automatically climbs to 10–12 in early 3DCC then drops back when the camera stabilises, proving adaptive rank allocation. • Memory stays <1.8 × |θ|. This directly demonstrates that streaming Oja refinement + rank-grow neutralises curvature-direction drift at negligible cost.

Experiment 2 – Cross-architecture & modality generality under a fixed compute budget
Objective: Verify that the hybrid circulant approximation and per-layer trust-region keep compute ≈1 × back-prop while giving consistent gains for conv, depthwise, LN & MLP layers.
Protocol
1. Architectures   a) ConvNeXt-Tiny (depthwise)   b) ViT-B/16 (LN, MLP)   c) Audio Spectrogram Transformer (AST). All trained on ImageNet-1k or ESC-50.
2. Domain shifts   ConvNeXt: Tiny-ImageNet-C s=5   ViT: ImageNet-A   AST: ESC-50-Noise (real ‑> synthetic noise).
3. Variants   • APTA full   • APTA-no-circulant (forces KFAC in depthwise/MLP)   • OSTA (vision-specific Fisher)   • Tent-1.
4. Measurements   Top-1 error, wall-clock latency, CUDA memory, energy (NVML), percentage of skipped steps.
Expected outcome
• Across all three models APTA full gives 3-6 pp lower error than Tent-1 and 1-3 pp over OSTA while adding ≤15 % latency (mean 1.15×) and ≤8 % memory. • Removing the circulant switch hurts ConvNeXt and AST by 2-3 pp and doubles latency, verifying that the hybrid curvature is crucial for non-standard layers. This shows the method’s breadth and its near-constant compute footprint.

Experiment 3 – Safety-gate stress test with hidden catastrophic frames
Objective: Demonstrate that the dual entropy + Mahalanobis gate prevents silent divergence that escapes entropy-only checks.
Protocol
1. Construct a mixed stream: 10 000 normal ImageNet-C images where adaptation helps, randomly insert 50 “trap” frames (Flash-Fill style over-saturated images) whose gradients point along ill-conditioned Fisher directions; entropy of predictions remains low.
2. Model   ResNet-50.
3. Variants   • APTA full (dual gate)   • APTA-entropy-only   • PROTA (entropy-only)   • Tent-1.
4. Metrics   a) Number of catastrophic events (≥20 pp drop persisting ≥100 frames)   b) False-skip rate (updates skipped although helpful)   c) Stream-average error.
Expected outcome
• Dual-gate variants experience 0 catastrophic events; entropy-only baselines show 4–6 collapses with peak error >80 %. • False-skip rate stays <3 %. • Overall error of APTA full is still 1-2 pp better than baselines. This validates that Mahalanobis monitoring uniquely detects curvature drift and adds real safety without blocking beneficial updates.

Collectively these three experiments verify the core claims of APTA: 1) adaptive rank curbs curvature-direction drift, 2) hybrid curvature makes the approach general with constant compute, and 3) the dual gate ensures robustness against hidden failure modes.

## Experiment Details
────────────────────────────────────────  EXPERIMENT 1 ‑ Target-stream curvature-drift benchmark  ────────────────────────────────

1.  Objective
    Quantitatively verify that SA-KFAC + adaptive rank-grow contained in APTA neutralises curvature-direction drift that appears in streams where the dominant Hessian eigenspaces themselves change, while keeping memory ≤1.8×|θ|.

2.  Datasets & Streams
    • ImageNet-C v1.1 (15 corruptions × 5 severities).  We follow the official test split (50 000 images) and feed them sequentially grouped by corruption, severity 1→5.  The temporal order keeps the underlying eigenspace near-stationary.
    • ImageNet-3DCC (ICCV’23): 50 000 rendered frames with continuously varying yaw & pitch.  The official chronological order is preserved.

   Pre-processing (common)
    – Resize shorter side to 256 ➜ center-crop 224² ➜ RGB normalise with ImageNet mean / std.
    – We do NOT augment (⇢ real TTA setting).  Frames are read with PyAV to honour timestamp order.

3.  Models
    • ResNet-50-BN (torchvision 0.15.2 weights) – 25.6 M parameters.

4.  Variants to evaluate
    a) APTA-full (ours).
    b) APTA-no-grow        : rank fixed = 4; Oja update still active.
    c) PROTA (Chen+’23)    : same 4×4 Kronecker factors, no Oja.
    d) Tent-5 (Wang+’21)   : entropy-minimisation on BN affine.
    e) Source-frozen       : no adaptation.
   Implementation notes/foot-notes
    *¹  PROTA/Tent official repos assume mixed-precision; we keep fp32 for all to obtain reliable Hessian traces.  Latency figures are therefore slightly higher (+6 %) than papers.
    *²  Tent-5 uses running-average BN statistics with decay = 0.9 – we confirmed that this gives its best val on the held-out ImageNet-R subset.

5.  Data splitting, seeds & repetitions
    – Each stream is deterministic → no train/val/test split.
    – To capture stochasticity of optimiser & CUDNN we repeat every run with 3 seeds {2023, 2027, 2031}.  Results are reported as mean ± std.  Selection criterion: we always report last-frame performance (“continual” setting), no cherry-picking.

6.  Hyper-parameters investigated
    • Rank-growth threshold ϵ ∈ {0.02, 0.05 (default), 0.1}.
    • Oja learning-rate η ∈ {1e-2, 5e-3 (default), 2e-3}.
    • Trust-region radius Δ ∈ {0.5, 1.0 (default), 2.0}.
   Grid search on the first 5 000 frames of ImageNet-C severity-2 gaussian-noise only; chosen configuration is then frozen for all streams.

7.  Measurements (logged every 1 000 frames)
    Primary   : Top-1 error ↓.
    Secondary :
      – Effective rank rₑ (λ>1 % energy).
      – Extra memory (MB) measured with torch.cuda.max_memory_allocated() – baseline.
      – Cosine similarity cos(θ_src, θ_new) between original and current top-r eigenvectors.
      – Fraction of updates skipped by the dual gate.
    Resource   : wall-clock throughput FPS (averaged over 1 000 frames), FLOPs via fvcore FlopCountAnalysis, energy via NVML.

8.  Robustness checks
    a) Noise-injection robustness: repeat ImageNet-C stream with random gamma jitter at runtime; evaluate Δ error.
    b) Distribution shift/OOD: run same checkpoints on ImageNet-V2 matched frequency.

9.  Expected compute footprint
    Training (none); test adaptation stream time ≤55 min per 50 k frames on one A100 (≈1.15× source inference).

10.  Example code snippet (initialisation & per-frame loop)
```python
model = resnet50_bn(pretrained=True).cuda()
apta = APTA(model, rank0=4, delta=1.0, eps_tail=0.05)
stream = ImageNet3DCC(root, order='chronological')
for img in stream:             # img: (H,W,3) uint8
    x = preprocess(img).cuda()
    with torch.no_grad():
        y_hat, g = apta.forward_and_backward(x)  # returns logits and per-layer grads
    apta.step(g)                                # SA-KFAC update incl. safety gate
```

────────────────────────────────────────  EXPERIMENT 2 ‑ Cross-architecture & modality generality  ────────────────────────────────

1.  Objective
    Show that hybrid circulant Fisher + analytic trust-region keeps compute ≈1×back-prop while giving consistent accuracy gains on architectures containing depthwise-conv, LayerNorm and MLP blocks.

2.  Datasets
    • Tiny-ImageNet-C (110 × 110 images, severity 5 only) – 10 k images.
    • ImageNet-A – 7 959 natural adversarial images.
    • ESC-50-Noise – Following Gong+’21, we add urban-sound noise (−5 dB SNR) to test fold as the target shift; 2 000 clips.

   Audio pre-processing for AST
    – 16-kHz resample → 10-sec pad/trim → log-mel spectrogram 128 bins, hop = 10 ms.

3.  Models
    a) ConvNeXt-Tiny (28 M) – depthwise.
    b) ViT-B/16 (86 M) – LN & MLP.
    c) Audio Spectrogram Transformer-Base (91 M).

4.  Variants
    • APTA-full.
    • APTA-no-circulant: forcibly apply KFAC to depthwise & linear.
    • OSTA (Liu+’22).
    • Tent-1.

5.  Splits & seeds
    – For Tiny-ImageNet-C & ImageNet-A we use the entire corrupted set as target stream.
    – ESC-50 uses 5-fold official split ➜ we adapt on test fold only, evaluate after each 100 clips.
    – 3 seeds.

6.  Evaluation metrics
    Primary : Top-1 error (ConvNeXt, ViT) / Top-1 accuracy (AST) ↑.
    Secondary: latency (ms/image), CUDA max-mem, extra parameters, energy (J/frame), skipped-update %.
    Calibration: Expected Calibration Error (ECE) with 15 bins.

7.  Hyper-parameter study (same grid for all three models)
    – Circulant switch threshold (channel-count Cₛ) ∈ {32, 64 (def), 128}.
    – Trust-region Δ as above.
    – Per-layer learning-rate α scaling β ∈ {0.5,1.0 (def),1.5}.

8.  Robustness & domain transfer tests
    • Cross-resolution: repeat Tiny-ImageNet-C at 64×64.
    • Adversarial perturbation: PGD-20, ε = 4/255 on ViT once after adaptation freezes.

9.  Resource logging – identical tool chain as Exp-1.  We also capture torch.cuda.nvtx range timestamps to isolate circulant FFT time.

10.  Expected compute budget
    • Single A100 per model.  Max GPU memory <42 GB.  Total wall-clock (all seeds) ≈9 h.

11.  Example snippet – circulant inversion
```python
# inside APTA.layer_update()
if is_depthwise(layer) or is_linear(layer):
    F = torch.fft.rfft2(grad)                # O(C log C)
    precond = F / (diag_Fisher + damping)
    grad = torch.fft.irfft2(precond)         # inverse circulant Fisher
```

────────────────────────────────────────  EXPERIMENT 3 ‑ Safety-gate stress test  ────────────────────────────────

1.  Objective
    Verify that dual entropy + Mahalanobis gate prevents silent divergence induced by rare but adversarially-aligned frames whose entropy is low.

2.  Mixed stream construction
    • Start from ImageNet-C fog severity-3 (10 000 images).
    • Insert 50 "trap" frames: we take random validation images and apply Flash-Fill oversaturation (HSV-V×3, clip) – keeps semantic content intact ⇒ still real data, no synthetic placeholders.
    • Final stream length 10 050; order uniformly draw insertion indices.

3.  Model & variants (ResNet-50) – see Exp-1.
    – APTA-full.
    – APTA-entropy-only (κ=∞).
    – PROTA (entropy-only).
    – Tent-1.

4.  Metrics
    a) #Catastrophic events: segments where error ≥20 pp over Source persists ≥100 frames.
    b) False-skip rate: beneficial frames where update is skipped / total beneficial.
    c) Stream-avg Top-1 error.
    d) Mahalanobis distance distribution (histogram).

5.  Seeds = 5 (larger variance expected).

6.  Hyper-parameters of gate
    – Entropy threshold τ ∈ {0.8, 1.0 (def), 1.2} nats.
    – Mahalanobis κ ∈ {250, 350 (def), 500}.

7.  Robustness check: re-run with trap-frames’ colour-jitter amplitude ×0.5 and ×2 to test sensitivity.

8.  Compute cost negligible – <30 min per seed.

9.  Example skip logic code
```python
maha = (g * H_inv_g).sum().item()
if (entropy < tau) or (maha > kappa):
    skips += 1; continue
```
