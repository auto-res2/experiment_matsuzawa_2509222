- Strategy: 1. Full-Pipeline Benchmark on Real Datasets
   Goal: Show that HASHPIPE-GAT delivers better speed/accuracy/memory than HiPPO-GAT and vanilla GAT under real workloads.
   Procedure:

   What success looks like:
     • ≥1.5× speed-up over HiPPO and ≥4× over vanilla on all datasets.
     • Equal or +1 pp accuracy on Products & Papers100M.
     • Memory ≤40 GB for 12-layer Papers100M; HiPPO exceeds 70 GB.
     • Hashing <10 % of forward time after epoch 3, proving temporal reuse.
   Demonstrates: real-world effectiveness, end-to-end efficiency and no accuracy loss.

2. Guarantee-Validation Micro-study
   Goal: Empirically verify the (ε,δ) error bound of CountSketch and the fidelity of Top-T edge selection.
   Procedure:
     • Synthetic graph with 3 M nodes, power-law degree up to 10⁶ so that exact attention scores fit in memory on A100 (use float16, batch-wise evaluation).
     • For one frozen mini-batch, compute ground-truth Q·K and exact Top-T for every node.
     • Run HASHPIPE-GAT with sketch dimensions s∈{16,32,48,64} and record:
         – Relative error ‖QK̂−QK‖/‖QK‖ for each pair.
         – Top-T recall (% of true Top-T edges kept after SimHash+cache).
         – Probability that error>ε for ε∈{0.05,0.1}.
     • Repeat over 100 random seeds to estimate δ.
   What success looks like:
     • Empirical P(error>ε) ≤δ where δ≈exp(−ε²s/3) matches theory.
     • With s=32, median relative error ≤7 %, 95th-percentile ≤12 %.
     • Top-T recall ≥95 % overall and ≥99 % for hubs (deg>10 k).
   Demonstrates: the advertised (ε,δ) guarantees and that probabilistic filters do not drop important edges.

3. Scalability & Hub-Stress Experiment
   Goal: Prove sub-logarithmic amortised cost and cache efficacy when extremely high-degree hubs are present.
   Procedure:
     • Generate scale-free graphs with identical node count (10 M) but varying maximum degree: 10⁴, 10⁵, 10⁶.
     • Instrument code to log (a) average number of hashed candidates; (b) per-node forward time; (c) PTKC hit-rate per epoch.
     • Run 5 epochs each with HiPPO-GAT and HASHPIPE-GAT (no training, forward pass only) on a single A100.

     • HiPPO forward time grows ≈log d; HASHPIPE flattens after epoch 2, staying within ±15 % across the three graphs.

   Demonstrates: sub-logarithmic complexity, effectiveness of historical cache, and robustness to extreme degree distributions.
