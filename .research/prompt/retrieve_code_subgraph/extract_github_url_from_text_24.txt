The methodology is primarily theoretical and empirical. The theoretical foundation involves deriving a conservation law for GATs with positive homogeneous activation functions (e.g., ReLU, LeakyReLU) based on the concept of rescale invariance. This law, specifically Theorem 2.2 and Corollary 2.3, quantifies a relationship between model parameters and their gradients under gradient flow, explaining the issue of hampered gradient propagation in unbalanced networks. Based on this insight, the paper proposes a balanced initialization scheme (Procedure 2.6) which involves: 1) Setting attention parameters to zero at initialization. 2) Scaling the randomly initialized feature weights such that the L2-norm balance (c=0 in Eq. 5) is achieved across layers, specifically by ensuring the squared L2-norm of incoming weights minus the squared L2-norm of attention weights equals the squared L2-norm of outgoing weights for each neuron. For feature weights, both Xavier initialization and a Looks-Linear-Orthogonal (LLortho) initialization are explored prior to balancing.
