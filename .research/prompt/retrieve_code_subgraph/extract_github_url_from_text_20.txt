# Paper Outline
The core methodology, Degree-Quant (DQ), is an architecture-agnostic approach for Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) that addresses inaccurate weight updates and unrepresentative quantization ranges in GNNs. DQ comprises two main components: 1) Stochastic Protection from Quantization, where nodes with high in-degree are stochastically protected from quantization during training, allowing their message passing, aggregation, and update phases to operate at full precision to ensure more accurate gradient flow. A node mask is generated by sampling from a Bernoulli distribution with probabilities `p` interpolated between `pmin` and `pmax` based on node in-degree ranking. Weights, however, are always quantized. Protection is disabled at test time. 2) Percentile Tracking of Quantization Ranges, which uses percentiles (clipping the top and bottom 0.1% of values) instead of min-max or momentum-based methods to determine quantization ranges. This makes ranges more representative of the majority of values, reducing rounding error due to infrequent outliers. The method supports uniform 8-bit (INT8) and 4-bit (INT4) quantization for weights and activations across GCN, GAT, and GIN architectures, which conform to the Message Passing Neural Networks (MPNN) paradigm.
