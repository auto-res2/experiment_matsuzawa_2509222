# Experiment Strategy
================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 1 – Zero-Extra-Forward Benchmark on Standard Image Corruptions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Quantitatively verify that SWIFTADAPT preserves (or improves) accuracy while incurring ≈1× forward cost, unlike prior TTA that need ≥2 passes.

1. Datasets & Models
   • ImageNet-C, CIFAR-10-C/100-C at 15 severity/shift combinations.
   • Architectures: ResNet-50, ConvNeXt-T, ViT-B/16.

2. Baselines
   Source (no TTA), TENT, CoTTA, OSHA, FLASHADAPT.

3. Metrics
   • Top-1 error (mean over corruptions).
   • Relative adaptation cost C(g)=t_adapt/t_source.
   • Wall-clock FPS and energy per image (nvidia-smi pwrdraw).

4. Protocol
   a) Load source-only weights on A100.
   b) Stream 100 k images in random corruption order (η=1 online).
   c) Measure start-to-end time and power; disable heterogeneous LAS pipeline for the baselines to isolate forward counts.

5. Expected Outcome
   • SWIFTADAPT: C(g)≈1.05, 5 % energy overhead, error ≤ FLASHADAPT (+/-0.3 pp).
   • FLASHADAPT: C(g)≈2.0.  TENT/CoTTA: C(g)≥6.
   Demonstrates that “zero-extra-forward” claim holds without sacrificing robustness.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXPERIMENT 2 – Uncertainty-Aware Abstention & Theoretical Bound Check
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Show that the variance head σ̂ supplies a reliable risk estimate, allowing compute savings with a provable accuracy upper-bound.

1. Dataset & Model
   • ImageNet-C (ResNet-50) + 50 k clean ImageNet images interleaved (to emulate varying shift intensity).

2. Procedure
   a) Run SWIFTADAPT with τ_skip ∈ {0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1.0} (percentage of blocks skipped based on σ̂).
   b) Log for every sample i: (σ̂_max,i, block-skip mask, top-1 correctness).
   c) Compute empirical ΔAcc(τ_skip)=Acc(0)−Acc(τ_skip).
   d) Plot ΔAcc against theoretical bound ε(τ_skip) given in paper.
   e) Measure GPU FLOPs & latency versus τ_skip.

3. Success Criteria
   • All empirical ΔAcc curves lie strictly below ε(τ_skip).
   • Skipping ≈30 % of blocks yields ≥10 % latency drop with <0.3 pp accuracy loss.
   Confirms the usefulness of uncertainty estimates and the soundness of the bound.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXPERIMENT 3 – Real-Time Video Stream & On-Device Fine-Tuning
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Stress-test the complete pipeline (LAS overlap + episodic fine-tuning) in a realistic 60 fps stream with evolving shifts.

1. Data Stream
   • ImageNet-Vid 30 fps clips → duplicate every frame to reach 60 fps.
   • Insert synthetic weather shift that gradually increases then disappears (fog→rain→clean) over 20 k frames.

2. Hardware & Runtime
   • Use A100 but enforce per-frame budget equal to Jetson Orin NX (≈16 ms) by sleeping; this preserves timing fidelity while keeping measurement simple.
   • Allow 0.5 ms per 128-frame window for Adam fine-tuning (as specified).

3. Baselines
   FLASHADAPT (pipelined), CoTTA (best sliding-window version), Source.

4. Logged Metrics (per frame, streamed to file)
   • Latency (GPU busy time + CPU overlap).
   • Realised FPS (must stay ≥60 for success).
   • Instantaneous accuracy (moving 256-frame average).
   • GPU/CPU utilisation.

5. Expected Outcome
   • SWIFTADAPT keeps mean latency ≤16.5 ms, maintains 92 % of offline accuracy across shift transitions.
   • FLASHADAPT misses budget after ~8 k frames (average 32 ms, FPS≈31).
   • CoTTA accuracy collapses to <70 % during severe fog due to slow convergence.
   Demonstrates the practical viability of the full pipeline under tight real-time constraints and non-stationary shifts.

================================================================================
Collectively, these three experiments examine SWIFTADAPT from complementary perspectives: (1) classical benchmark accuracy vs speed, (2) correctness of its uncertainty-driven compute savings, and (3) end-to-end behaviour in real-world streaming conditions. Together they furnish compelling evidence of the method’s superiority and practicality.
